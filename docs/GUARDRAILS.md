# Guardrails and known limitations

Each guardrail: what triggers it, what happens, the threshold, why, whether the threshold is an **ASSUMPTION** (a team choice or proxy), a **RULE** (an insurance rule or regulation as used in our research; verify before relying on it) or a **DESIGN** rule (structural, not a number), the file that enforces it, and the test or attack that exercises it. "Held" means the test passes; the tests were written against the same code, so they show these cases fail, not that no other case exists. Paths are relative to the repository root.

## Known limitations (plain words)

- **Recall on hard photos is low.** On 41 public dev photos (mostly non-Indian cars, wide shots, not the protocol photos the system asks for) the models find 22 of 74 labelled damaged parts. 25 of those 74 parts were never outlined at all. The labels were made by Claude by eye, and the photos were also used for design choices, so this is an estimate, not an accuracy guarantee.
- **Silent misses.** In 8 of 74 cases a labelled damaged part was missed inside a claim that still ended in an OFFER, so that offer under-pays. The failure direction is under-payment; the quote dispute is the remedy, and no rule drops a detection or lowers an offer.
- **No scratch class.** The damage detector used at run time has no scratch class. A scratch-only photo gets ASK_MORE twice and then ESCALATE; a scratch next to other damage is left out of the offer.
- **Plates are typed.** The demo takes the number plate as typed text. Reading a plate photo needs the language model and was not measured on real plates.
- **Video is validated on synthetic clips only.** Nothing about real walk-around video is measured. The continuity threshold (15 inliers) and the 60-second and 12-keyframe limits are assumptions.
- **Quotes are synthetic.** Quote reading was measured on 15 synthetic printed quotes (45 of 53 lines exact, 15 of 15 grand totals). Handwritten quotes and real invoices are not measured.
- **No severity ground truth.** Severity bands come from the share of the part covered by damage; they agree with hand labels on 20 of 32 correctly located parts. Severity accuracy is not measured.
- **Prices are estimates.** Part prices are listed MRPs (151 rate-card rows); labour and paint are estimated from garage price ranges. Other-generation and similar-car prices are labelled IMPUTED. GST is treated as included (an open question).
- **Unseen photos.** On 8 unseen photos the system made 0 offers (`run1/DECK_NUMBERS.md`). That is the guardrails working, but it also means the system has never been shown to reach an offer on a photo it had not been designed around.
- **The adjuster view has no authentication.** It is labelled demo-only. There is no real adjuster queue.
- **Not assessed:** regulation and data-protection compliance, hosting cost, performance under load.
